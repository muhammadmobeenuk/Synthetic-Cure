# Approved Synthetic Cure visual rules

Source: the desktop and mobile mockups approved in the conversation on 27 October 2026. Preserve their identity: dark teal navigation and header, warm light operational surfaces, deep navy text, teal primary actions, restrained gold highlights, fine borders and simple line icons. Prioritize identifiable work, locations, quantities and next actions. Use text plus colour for status. Synthetic data must remain explicitly labelled.

Maintain existing navigation labels, role restrictions and real commands. Every button must reflect available behavior. Do not convert the illustrative mockup's figures, businesses, delivery outcomes, inferred quantities, pallet capacities, tracking marks, photos, routes or addresses into operational data. No automatic recurring orders. Preparation is not delivery. Distinguish order customer from recipient and location. Do not present local drafts as server confirmed.

Keep clear focus, semantic labels, readable contrast, 48px action targets where practical, wrapping text, mobile cards, and reduced motion. No glass behind operational text. Avoid changing or removing user workflows to match mockup geometry. New product decisions or substantive departures from the approved design require user clarification.

The 12ui image-to-code service requested sign-in; the shared shell was aligned manually against the approved image. Pixel-level 12ui conversion and full screen-by-screen fidelity have not been verified.
